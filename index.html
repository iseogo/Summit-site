About Us

Healthcare Logistics
Done Right

Summit Healthcare Logistics LLC is a professional medical courier company founded in Omaha, Nebraska. We exist to solve one of healthcare's most underestimated challenges — the reliable, secure, and time-sensitive movement of critical medical materials between facilities.

Compliance-First Mindset HIPAA-certified, OSHA-trained, and built around healthcare regulations from day one.

Our Services

What We Transport & Deliver

From biological specimens to medical equipment — we handle every type of healthcare material with the professionalism and care it demands.

Laboratory Specimens

Blood draws, urine samples, biopsies, and culture swabs transported in temperature-controlled, biohazard-safe containers with full chain of custody documentation.

Pharmaceutical Delivery

Prescription medications, controlled substances, and specialty drugs delivered securely between pharmacies, hospitals, and patients with verified handling protocols.

Medical Supplies

IV fluids, dressing kits, sterile supplies, PPE, and consumables delivered on-demand to clinics, home health agencies, and long-term care facilities.

Healthcare Documents

Patient records, lab reports, insurance authorizations, and legal medical documents transported with strict confidentiality and tamper-evident packaging.

Small Medical Equipment

Portable monitoring devices, diagnostic tools, infusion pumps, and durable medical equipment relocated between facilities or delivered to patients at home.

Scheduled & Stat Runs

Routine daily pickups, STAT urgent deliveries, and after-hours courier service — flexible scheduling designed around your facility's operational needs.
